Badminton



Badminton uses the rally point system, where every rally earns a point and each game is played to 21 points with a required two point lead

A legal serve must be hit diagonally below the waist, and faults—such as the shuttle landing out of bounds or a player touching the net—give the point to the opponent

Shuttle landing out, failing to cross the net, double hits, or touching the net results in a fault and point to the opponent
